## What this is

- A statistical model that estimates the probability of an upcoming paper
  reprint, based on public catalog data, historical reprint patterns, and
  secondary-market price behavior.
- The model produces probabilities, not certainties. "92% likely" means the
  model has historically been right roughly that often on similar cards; it
  is not a guarantee.

## What this is not

- It is **not** insider information from Wizards of the Coast.
- It is **not** a forecast of future card prices.
- It is **not** a substitute for your own judgment or for professional advice.
- It does **not** account for your individual financial situation,
  collection, tax position, or risk tolerance.

## Honest limitations

- Reprint decisions are made by Wizards of the Coast and are not public. The
  model has no information about unannounced products.
- Past predictive accuracy does not guarantee future accuracy.
- Markets often price in obvious reprint risk before any model can; the edge
  comes from cases where the model disagrees with the consensus, and those
  are also the cases where the model is most likely to be wrong.
- Collectible markets are illiquid and can have wide buy/sell spreads;
  transaction costs can erase predicted savings.
