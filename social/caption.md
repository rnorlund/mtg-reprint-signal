## Short post

I built an AI that predicts MTG reprints.

I trained it on every paper printing since 1993 plus 16 years of daily prices, then tested it honestly: back in June 2024 it ranked the 100 cards most likely to be reprinted within a year.

96 of those 100 actually got reprinted. Random guessing would land about 15.

It nailed the obvious ones (basic lands, Sol Ring, the bouncelands) and whiffed on a few surprises (Doubling Season, Counterspell). No insider info, just patterns: Commander popularity, time since last printing, and the release calendar.

What card do you think is overdue for a reprint? 👀
